What Is Management?

A simple, complete introduction to management — what it means, what managers do, and why it matters in every organization.

Management in simple words

Management is the practice of coordinating people, resources, time and goals so an organization can create value. It includes planning what should happen, organizing resources, leading people, and controlling progress.

Frequently asked questions

Is management the same as leadership?

No. Leadership is one function of management (the “leading” part). Management also includes planning, organizing and controlling.

Why do organizations need management?

Management keeps people aligned, resources organized and progress visible so teams can achieve shared goals.
