How the Human Heart Works

A simple tour of the heart, blood flow and the circulatory system.

Health & Medical

Simple explanation

The heart is a muscular pump that moves blood through the lungs and the rest of the body.

Blood carries oxygen and nutrients to cells and helps remove waste products.
