What Is Management?

Definitions, the four core functions, and why management matters in every organization.

Business & Management

Simple explanation

Management is the process of coordinating people and resources to achieve organizational goals effectively and efficiently.

The four core functions are planning, organizing, leading and controlling.

Quick quiz

Which function sets goals? Planning
Which function motivates people? Leading
