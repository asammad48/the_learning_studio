Understanding Supply Chains

From raw materials to the customer — the journey every product takes.

Supply Chain & Operations

Simple explanation

A supply chain is the network of people, organizations, resources and activities that move a product from origin to customer.

Good supply chains balance cost, speed, quality and reliability.
